Install the app
One tap from your home screen, full screen.

    Your teams' matches with kick-off and where to watch, kept up to date. iPhone: tap Subscribe. Android: tap Copy link, then in Google Calendar on a computer choose Other calendars → From URL and paste it (Google updates it about once a day).

    My competitions

    Football from the others is hidden everywhere. Your favorite teams' matches always show.

    Filters and display

    Why hide names? In a tournament, just seeing who plays a later round tells you who won earlier. Tap a hidden name to reveal it.

    Custom scoring (football)

    Decide what makes a match exciting for you. Your scores are worked out on this device only; everyone else sees the standard scores.

    The four pillars

    Each pillar scores the match from 0 to 10; the match score is their weighted average. Set how much each counts.

    Advanced: the parts of each pillar

    In points per match. These can raise or lower all the scores.

    Boost or lower competitions

    Points added to every match in that competition, before the top end.
